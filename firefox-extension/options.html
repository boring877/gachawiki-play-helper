DMM Region Fix — settings

Cookies (always on)

Nothing to configure. The extension keeps ckcy=1, cklg=welcome and age_check_done=1 pinned on dmm.com and dmm.co.jp, and instantly restores them if DMM overwrites or deletes them.

Japan proxy for DMM traffic (optional)

If the cookies alone don't get you in (DMM sometimes hard-checks the IP), put a Japan proxy here and only DMM/FANZA traffic will go through it — the rest of your browsing stays on your normal connection.

⚠ An extension cannot create a Japanese IP by itself — you must get the proxy from a service you trust (your VPN provider's proxy/SOCKS, or your own Japan VPS). Never use proxies from random free lists: your DMM login passes through them and can be stolen.

Where to get a Japan proxy: many VPN subscriptions include one (check your provider's app for SOCKS/proxy details). Free options that are reputable: ProtonVPN (free plan, Japan server — as a whole-device VPN), Windscribe (free 10 GB/month, includes proxy). Or rent a cheap Japan VPS and run a SOCKS5 proxy on it.

Reminder: region-spoofing breaks DMM's terms of service — use at your own risk, and avoid charging money while it's active.
